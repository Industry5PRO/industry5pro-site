Common Questions & Architectural Philosophy

"Why shouldn't I just bundle CCaaS with my Carrier?"

Because carriers sell networks, not customer experiences. When you bundle, you often get a generic, "white-labeled" version of a platform (like RingCentral or Genesys) with limited features and slow support. We decouple the **Network** (Lumen Voice) from the **Platform** (NICE/Five9), giving you the best transport and the best features, without the "carrier handcuffs."

"What about our elevators and fire alarms?"

This is the #1 blocker for cloud migrations. You can't move a fire panel to Zoom. That is why we architect a parallel track: high-speed cloud voice for users, and **Lumen Specialty Lines** (or LTE/5G POTS Replacement) for critical analog devices. We solve the "unsexy" infrastructure so your cloud project doesn't stall.

"Why use Industry5 instead of going direct?"
