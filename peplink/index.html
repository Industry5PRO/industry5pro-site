Common Questions & Architectural Philosophy

"Can we really run our whole business on wireless?"

Yes, but only if you bond it. A single 5G connection is a point of failure. We use Peplink SpeedFusion to bond multiple disparate links (Verizon 5G + Starlink + Local Broadband) into a single, unbreakable pipe. If one carrier drops, your video call doesn't even flicker.

"Why do we need an architect? Can't we just buy Starlink?"

Starlink is a fantastic circuit, but it's not a corporate network. It lacks static IPs, inbound routing, and granular traffic control. We wrap satellite connections in an SD-WAN overlay, giving you the reach of space with the security and control of a private MPLS line.

"Won't 5G data overages kill our budget?"

Not with Traffic Steering. We configure your edge policies to route bulk traffic (like OS updates or guest Wi-Fi) to cheap wireline or standard broadband, while reserving the high-speed, low-latency cellular path exclusively for mission-critical apps (VoIP, POS, ERP). You pay for performance where it counts.
