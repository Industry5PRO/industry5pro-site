Cyber Resilience Overlay — How to Read This Quadrant

There isn’t one vendor that truly does it all across Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, and Govern & Comply at an enterprise level without tradeoffs. Resilience comes from a simple backbone: identity at the front door, data/telemetry as truth, and recoverability as the safety net, then layering best of breed where it matters. Think separate failure domains, clean integrations, and SLAs you can defend.

  • Protect: Zero trust, WAAP/WAF, email/endpoint hardening.
  • Detect: 24×7 MDR/XDR coverage and fast MTTR.
  • Respond: Runbooks, comms, forensics, and ownership.
  • Recover: Immutability, instant recovery, and DRaaS testing.
  • Govern & Comply: Policies, evidence, retention, third-party risk.
