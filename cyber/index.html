Common Questions & Architectural Philosophy

"We already have a SOC and EDR. Aren't we safe?"

You are protected, but not necessarily resilient. Security tools are designed to stop the entry. Resilience is designed to survive the breach. When an attacker uses valid credentials (which happens in 80% of breaches), your EDR won't fire. That is why we build an Immutable Air Gap—so you can recover even when the "front door" fails.

"Why do we need a 'Network' architect for backups?"

Physics. Most companies have a "Vault" but a tiny "Pipe." Recovering 500TB of data over a standard 1Gbps internet connection takes weeks. We align Lumen's high-bandwidth RapidRoutes with Commvault's Cleanroom Recovery to ensure you can re-hydrate your production environment in minutes, not months.

"Why engage Industry5 instead of going direct?"

If you go direct, you get two siloed sales teams: one selling fiber, one selling software. Neither cares about your Recovery Time Objective (RTO). We sit in the middle, architecting the entire recovery workflow to ensure the network and storage perform as a single survival engine.
