Common Questions & Architectural Philosophy

"Is NaaS just a fancy word for MPLS?"

No. MPLS is static; you buy it on a 3-year contract and wait 90 days to install. Lumen NaaS is elastic. You spin up a 10G private connection to AWS for an hour to move a massive dataset, then spin it down. You pay for the *capacity you use*, not the port size you installed.

"How do we stop bleeding money on Cloud Egress?"

Public internet egress fees are the silent killer of cloud budgets. By routing traffic through a private NaaS fabric (like Lumen's), you bypass the public internet entirely. This often reduces egress rates by 40-60% while simultaneously dropping latency by 30ms. It pays for itself.

"Do we really need Edge Compute?"

If your application needs to make a decision in under 10ms (like visual AI or robotic control), the round trip to `us-east-1` is too slow. We deploy Bare Metal at the metro edge—putting the compute within 5ms of your users—so you get the speed of on-prem with the flexibility of the cloud.
