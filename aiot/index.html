Manufacturing — Siemens Insights Hub + Industrial Edge

When you’re trying to bring legacy OT, modern PLCs, and cloud analytics into the same conversation, Siemens is usually on my shortlist. Insights Hub gives you a cloud-based industrial IoT layer to collect, normalize, and visualize data from lines, cells, and plants. Industrial Edge pushes analytics down to the line so you can run AI inference close to the machines, even when the WAN is having a bad day.
