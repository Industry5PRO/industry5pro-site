Common Questions & Architectural Philosophy

"Do we need to rip and replace our legacy machines?"

Rarely. The goal of Industrial Edge (Siemens) and AIoT overlay (EPIC iO) is to extract intelligence from existing assets. We use non-intrusive sensors (vibration, temperature, optical) and edge gateways that clamp onto your existing brownfield environment. We modernize the data stream, not the metal.

"Why shouldn't I just go direct to Verizon or AT&T?"

Carriers sell coverage maps; we sell connection success rates. If you go direct, you are locked into one network's dead zones. By using Aeris or Peplink through Industry5, we build a multi-carrier fabric. Your devices automatically switch to whichever signal is strongest, and you get a single pane of glass for billing and management.

"How does the engagement model work?"

We are paid by the suppliers (Siemens, Aeris, etc.) to be your architect. You pay the same (or often less due to our volume contracts) than you would going direct, but you get an unbiased architecture that aligns the right tech to your P&L, not a sales quota.
